# Papyrus 0.4.0

6 November 2026 · Build 8

Papyrus 0.4.0 helps you make time for reading and understand your reading habits, with a new reading timer, clearer statistics, and easier-to-navigate settings.

## Make time for a book

- Open **Library → Timer**, choose a book, and start a stopwatch or a countdown. Pick 15, 30, or 60 minutes, or set your own duration.
- Switch to **Focus** for a full-screen clock. Pause and resume when you need a break, then save your reading time with an optional page update.
- Review your recent sessions alongside the timer. Saved time contributes to your reading statistics and goals.
- Timers work offline. When you are signed in and connected, your active timer is shared across your devices. Guest timers stay on the device.
- On Android, pause, resume, or finish a session from the ongoing notification without returning to the app.

## Understand your reading habits

- See reading time, recorded pages, books finished, and reading days together in the redesigned **Statistics** page.
- Switch between time and pages, explore your daily rhythm and reading streaks, and compare weeks, months, years, or a custom date range.
- See which genres you have been reading. You can now edit a book’s genre in its details.
- EPUB page tracking now records individual displayed pages, giving a more precise picture of what you read while preserving existing history.

## Find settings more easily

- Mobile **Settings** now groups options into Account, Appearance, Storage, Accessibility, and About, each with its own page.
- **App updates** has a dedicated page with clearer progress and retry feedback. Storage usage is easier to scan, with a progress bar and compact actions.
- Choose left-aligned or centered page content in **Appearance**.

## A clearer library

- Book and shelf cards stand out more clearly in dark mode. Favorite and selection icons are easier to see across light, dark, high-contrast, and e-ink themes.
- Folder cards and details use space more efficiently. Reconnect actions are easier to find, quick folder previews show less loading flicker, and Folders and Catalogs have consistent page headings.
- Settings, charts, and library layouts handle narrow screens and larger text more comfortably.

## Updating

Update through your current installation source. Android testers using Google Play should use the internal-testing update. If you installed the APK directly, use the new APK from this release. Your library and existing reading history are retained.
